# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Fenrir Agent community harassment-free
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity or expression, experience level, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while discussing code, models, and security boundaries.
- Welcome questions from people with different experience levels.
- Keep criticism focused on ideas and implementations, never people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing private
information without permission, threats, and sustained disruption are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `mnisperuza1102@gmail.com`. Reports will
be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers
may remove content, issue a warning, or restrict participation when needed to
protect the community.

This policy applies in project spaces and at public events where someone is
representing Fenrir Agent.
